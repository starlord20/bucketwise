# Demo Video Script — Bucketwise (2:30) + Pitch Beats

Record happy path twice, no music, clean voiceover. Target: a cold viewer can
repeat back what Bucketwise does after one watch.

## 0:00–0:15 Hook (face or dashboard + voice)
> "70 million US families manage college savings, emergency buffers, and life
> events across six different apps. I live this in Center Valley, PA. Charts
> never changed my behavior — so I built Bucketwise: Financial Freedom OS.
> Actions, not charts."

## 0:15–1:45 Live Workflow (screen capture, no cuts mid-flow)
1. **Upload (0:15–0:35):** "Here's 98 real transactions across 90 days." Upload `sample-bank.csv`.
   Point at the staged progress: reading → parsing → Wonder → Spark → digest.
2. **3 Buckets (0:35–0:55):** "Wonder allocates every dollar: Emergency $900 of
   $15k, College $2,550 of $250k, Life Event $600 of $30k — each stamped with
   its source and sync time."
3. **Leaks (0:55–1:10):** "Spark caught what I never would: Adobe billed twice
   under two names, Spotify Family overlapping Spotify — $69.98 a month, $840
   a year. Monthly bills are never flagged — only real duplicates."
4. **Receipt (1:10–1:20):** "A paper receipt? Scan it — SAM 3.1 parses it and
   one tap adds it to the buckets."
5. **Shock-test (1:20–1:45):** "Now the fear test: what if 2020 repeats?
   Income −30%." Run it. Read one verdict: "FAIL — $900 covers 6% of the
   buffer. Pause college, fund emergency first."

## 1:45–2:20 Impact (digest on screen)
> "Every morning Spark writes three dollar-amount actions — cancel this, move
> that. Found: $69.98/mo leaks. Buffer plan if 2020 repeats. Three buckets,
> auto-updated. Built on four Meta models — Wonder, Spark, SAM, and Image —
> through one Meta Muse API."

## 2:20–2:30 Close (logo card)
