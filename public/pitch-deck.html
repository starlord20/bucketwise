Karthik Ramani · Center Valley, PA · Team Bucketwise

01 · Problem

70M US families run the household across 6+ apps.

  • Bank statements, Gmail receipts, 529 emails, Drive tax docs — nowhere together.
  • Existing tools give charts. Families need to survive the next shock.
  • Families with a buffer + a simple plan survived 2008 / 2020 / 2022 better. Families without didn't.

02 · Solution

Upload → Parse → 3 Buckets → Shock-Test → Daily Digest

Wonder allocates every dollar · Spark finds leaks + writes the digest · Wonder shock-tests history.

03 · Proof — real numbers, our own test

$69.98/mo recovered · $839.76/yr

  • Adobe billed twice under two names + Spotify Family overlapping Spotify — traced to named charges, with dates.
  • Monthly bills are never flagged — only 3-day clusters and variant names.
  • Full run ≈ 5k tokens. Blended cost per family: pennies per month.
